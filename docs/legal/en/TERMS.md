# TradeDoc AI Terms of Use

> **Draft.** Not a legally reviewed text. Before publication: [check with a lawyer]. Revision: 2026-11-03.

1. **What it is.** TradeDoc AI helps find differences between trade documents (invoice, packing list, CMR) before
   the cargo leaves. It shows what is written where and drafts a letter asking the responsible party to confirm
   the correct values.
2. **What it is not.** TradeDoc AI is not a customs broker or customs representative, not a lawyer and not an
   adviser. It does not file declarations, does not make decisions for the user and does not guarantee border
   clearance or the absence of delays, fines or claims.
3. **Responsibility for documents.** The user and the party that issued a document are responsible for the final
   documents, their content and their submission. Every finding must be checked against the originals.
4. **AI can be wrong.** Part of the document reading may be done by an external AI model. Its results may be
   incomplete or wrong. Fields read from a photo always need a manual check. The comparison is done by code, but
   only on what could be read.
5. **No legal advice.** Inspector questions and letter texts are for reference only and are not legal, tax or
   customs advice. Rules without a stated source and check date are marked "not verified" and are not statements
   about legal requirements.
6. **Demo mode.** With a free model (Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter) only synthetic (fictional) documents may be
   uploaded: on free tiers the provider may use the data to improve its products.
7. **Your data and third-party data.** Upload only documents you have a basis to process. See the Privacy Policy
   and the Data Processing Consent.
