# TradeDoc AI Privacy Policy

> **Draft.** Before publication: [check with a lawyer]. Revision: 2026-11-03.

**Data processed.** Texts and images of trade documents you upload or paste. They may contain personal data:
names of drivers and representatives, contact details, document numbers.

**Where it is kept and for how long.**

| Where | What | How long |
|---|---|---|
| TradeDoc AI server | document file and text | only while the request is processed, in memory; never written to disk, a database or logs |
| Server, demo-mode cache | reading result of a synthetic document by a free model | up to 30 minutes in memory; free-model mode only; no cache for Meta |
| Your browser (localStorage) | the shipment case: document texts, versions, check history; consent; language | until you delete it: the "Delete all my data" button |
| External AI provider | document text (personal data masked) or image | under the provider's rules, see below |

**Transfer to external AI providers.** When a model mode is on, the document is sent to the provider (Google,
Groq, OpenRouter or Meta), whose servers may be outside your country. This may be a cross-border transfer:
[check with a lawyer]. Before sending text, the service hides IIN/BIN, phone numbers, e-mails, IBANs, passport
numbers and names on "Driver" and "Representative" lines. Images are sent as they are. On free tiers the provider
may use the data to improve its products and show it to reviewers, so only synthetic documents are allowed there.

**Logs.** The server writes only service lines (start, fallback mode). Document texts, keys and personal data are
never logged.

**Cookies and external resources.** No cookies. No external fonts or scripts are loaded.

**Your rights.** You can delete all data in the browser at any time with "Delete all my data". No documents of
yours remain on the server after the response. Other data-subject rights: [check with a lawyer].
